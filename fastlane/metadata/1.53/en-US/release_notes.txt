Version 1.53 makes voice scoring feel more natural: press and hold the microphone, say the complete play, then release to process it as one instruction. Keep your attention on the court while you record.

We also improved player-name recognition, combined plays, period-end actions, and team and player management. You can now add overtime and keep recording every play. Voice scoring is available in 10 languages.

Game and player groups now sync with iCloud across your devices, while remaining compatible with existing group data.

Restore teams and players from saved games in Settings > Data Management.

Excel event details now reflect deleted, restored, and edited plays in both game and player reports. You can also clear a player's photo while editing.

Career trends are easier to compare with metric-specific reference points and ranges. Player and team photos are processed in the background with a progress indicator.
